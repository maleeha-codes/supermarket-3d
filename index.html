Welcome to SuperMart 3D

A professional first-person 3D supermarket shopping experience. Your trolley is attached in front of you and follows automatically.

🖱️ Look Around Click & drag mouse (or drag right side on mobile).
🚶 Walk Aisles Use WASD / Arrow keys or the mobile virtual joystick.
🍎 Pick Items Hover over any product and click/tap to drop it into your trolley.
💳 5% GST Billing Walk to the front counter and press E to get your bill.
Click Start to begin your shopping trip

🛒 Supermarket Controls

🖱️
Looking Around (360°)

Click & hold anywhere on screen, then drag your mouse to look around freely. On mobile/tablet, swipe on the right half of the screen.

🚶
Walking & Movement

Use W, A, S, D or Arrow keys on desktop. On phones/tablets, use the on-screen virtual joystick on the bottom-left.

🛒
Attached Trolley (Always in Front)

Your trolley stays in front of you wherever you walk. Picked items automatically drop into the basket and stack inside it.

🍎
Picking Products (3.5m Reach)

Hover over any item on the shelves to view its name and ₹ price. A single click adds it directly to your trolley.

💳
Checkout & 5% GST Receipt

Walk to the front billing counter (Lane 1) and press E or click Checkout in your trolley drawer to see your final bill.
